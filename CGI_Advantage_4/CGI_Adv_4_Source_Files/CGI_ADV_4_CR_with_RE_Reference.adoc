=== Overview
This guide provides a step-by-step process for creating a **CR (Cash Receipt)** transaction in CGI Advantage 4 that references an **RE (Receivable Entry)** event. Follow these instructions to apply payments against receivable entries accurately.

==== Step 1: Verify the RE (Receivable Entry) Event

. **Navigate to Receivable Entry (RE)**
  - Go to **Financials** > **Revenue** > **Receivable Entry (RE)**.
. **Search for the RE Transaction**
  - Enter relevant search criteria, such as **Transaction ID**, **Customer ID**, or **Date**.
  - Click **Search** to locate the RE transaction you want to reference.
. **Verify the RE Details**
  - Open the RE transaction and verify details like **Customer ID**, **Amount Due**, **Outstanding Balance**, and **Payment Terms**.
  - Note the **Transaction ID** and **Line Item IDs** (if applicable) for later reference in the CR transaction.

==== Step 2: Create the Cash Receipt (CR) Transaction

. **Navigate to Cash Receipt (CR)**
  - Go to **Financials** > **Cash Management** > **Cash Receipt (CR)**.
. **Create New CR Transaction**
  - Click **Create New** to start a new Cash Receipt transaction.
. **Enter Header Information**
  - **Transaction Date**: Enter the date the payment was received.
  - **Receipt Number**: Assign a unique receipt number, or use the system-generated one if available.
  - **Customer ID**: Enter the **Customer ID** that matches the one used in the RE transaction.
  - **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method, such as **Check**, **ACH**, or **Cash**.
  - **Amount Received**: Enter the total amount received from the customer.
  - **Bank Account**: Select the bank account where the funds will be deposited.

==== Step 3: Reference the RE Transaction in CR Line Items

. **Add Line Item for the Cash Receipt**
  - In the **Line Items** section, click **Add Line Item**.
. **Enter Line Item Details**
  - **Receivable Event ID**: Enter the **Transaction ID** of the RE event that this payment will apply to.
  - **Line Item ID (if applicable)**: If the RE transaction includes multiple line items, specify the exact **Line Item ID** to which this payment should apply.
  - **Amount Applied**: Enter the amount to be applied to the RE event. This amount can match the outstanding balance or be a partial payment.
. **Add Additional Line Items (if needed)**
  - If the payment applies to multiple RE events or line items, repeat the process for each line item by referencing the appropriate **Receivable Event ID** and **Line Item ID**.

==== Step 4: Review and Validate the Cash Receipt Transaction

. **Validate the Cash Receipt (CR) Entry**
  - Ensure that the **Amount Received** matches the total of all line items.
  - Double-check that each **Receivable Event ID** and **Amount Applied** aligns with the correct RE transactions.
. **Check for Errors or Warnings**
  - CGI Advantage 4 will display any validation errors or warnings. Address these before proceeding.

==== Step 5: Submit or Approve the CR Transaction

. **Save the Transaction**
  - Click **Save** to save the transaction details.
. **Submit the Cash Receipt (CR) for Approval**
  - If your organization requires approval for cash receipts, submit the CR transaction by clicking **Submit**.
  - The transaction will then be routed through the approval workflow as per your organization’s configuration.
. **Finalize the Transaction**
  - Once approved (if required), the CR transaction will be posted, and the payment will be applied against the RE receivable, reducing the outstanding balance on the customer’s account.

==== Step 6: Verify Application of Payment

. **Check the RE Transaction Status**
  - After posting, return to the **Receivable Entry (RE)** module.
  - Search for the original RE transaction and open it to verify that the payment has been applied correctly.
. **Confirm Updated Balance**
  - Review the **Outstanding Balance** on the RE transaction. The applied payment should reduce this balance accordingly.
. **Generate or Print Receipt (if needed)**
  - If your organization requires a printed receipt, use the **Print Receipt** option in the CR transaction or generate a report to send to the customer.

==== Additional Notes

- **Partial Payments**: If a partial payment is applied, the outstanding balance on the RE transaction will reflect the remaining amount due.
- **Overpayments**: If the payment exceeds the amount due, the system may generate a credit balance or an adjustment, depending on configuration.
- **Audit and Review**: Ensure that all CR and RE transactions align with the customer’s account details for accurate reporting and audit purposes.
