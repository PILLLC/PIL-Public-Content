=== Overview
This document details the architecture, strategy, and plan for implementing end-user training for CGI Advantage 4’s **Financial Management (FIN)** and **Human Resources Management (HRM)** modules. The goal is to equip end-users with the skills and knowledge required to perform their job functions effectively within the system. The training plan covers foundational knowledge, role-specific workflows, and practical application through hands-on exercises.

=== 1. Training Architecture

The training architecture is structured around a blended learning model, combining **classroom training**, **e-learning modules**, **hands-on labs**, and **reference materials**.

==== Training Delivery Modes
[cols="2,5", options="header"]
|===
| Delivery Mode         | Description

| Classroom Training    | Instructor-led sessions focusing on core concepts, system navigation, and basic functionalities. Ideal for onboarding and introducing key concepts.
| E-Learning Modules    | Self-paced online modules covering each function in detail. Allows users to revisit content as needed and provides flexibility for remote users.
| Hands-On Labs         | Practical exercises where users perform tasks in a sandbox environment. Enables application of knowledge in a low-risk setting.
| Job Aids and Manuals  | Reference materials including quick reference guides, job aids, and user manuals. Supports users in the workplace with quick look-up resources.
|===

==== Training Curriculum Design
The curriculum is designed to align with core functional areas of the FIN and HRM modules, organized into **foundation training**, **role-specific training**, and **advanced feature training**:

1. **Foundation Training**: Core functionalities, navigation, and common features across FIN and HRM modules.
2. **Role-Specific Training**: Tailored to roles such as finance officers, HR managers, payroll clerks, and departmental administrators.
3. **Advanced Features**: Covers complex functions such as budgeting adjustments, multi-entity financial reporting, payroll reconciliation, and benefit management.

=== 2. Training Strategy

The training strategy is focused on maximizing user engagement, reinforcing learning through hands-on practice, and ensuring knowledge retention over time. This includes:

==== Objectives
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Ensure users understand system functionality and core processes.
- **Skill Development**: Build user confidence and proficiency in executing specific tasks.
- **Retention and Application**: Reinforce learning through job aids, practice, and ongoing support.

==== Key Elements
1. **Targeted Needs Assessment**: Conduct a needs assessment to identify knowledge gaps, job-specific requirements, and user proficiency levels.
2. **Curriculum Customization**: Tailor training modules based on assessment findings and align content with actual workflows and job requirements.
3. **Evaluation and Feedback**: Implement pre- and post-assessments, quizzes, and surveys to measure user progress and adapt training as necessary.

==== Learning Paths by Role
[cols="3,5", options="header"]
|===
| Role              | Learning Path

| Finance Officer   | Foundational FIN training, AP/AR functions, reporting, and financial reconciliation.
| HR Manager        | Foundational HRM training, employee records management, benefits administration, and reporting.
| Payroll Clerk     | Payroll setup, payroll processing, tax configuration, and payroll reconciliation.
| Departmental Admin | Navigation, report generation, basic data entry, and cross-departmental workflows.
|===

=== 3. Training Plan

The training plan includes preparation, delivery, and post-training support to ensure successful adoption. Each phase is designed to build knowledge, reinforce skills, and provide continuous support for optimal system utilization.

==== Phase 1: Training Preparation

1. **Develop Training Materials**:
   - Prepare e-learning modules, slides, job aids, and user manuals.
   - Customize materials for CGI Advantage 4 FIN and HRM modules to reflect specific workflows and configurations.
   
2. **Set Up Training Environment**:
   - Establish a sandbox or training environment in CGI Advantage 4 for hands-on exercises.
   - Load realistic sample data for end-to-end testing of functions like invoice processing, payroll, and reporting.

3. **Assign Training Schedules**:
   - Schedule sessions according to role and proficiency level.
   - Notify users of their assigned training sessions and provide pre-training resources to set expectations.

==== Phase 2: Training Delivery

===== Module 1: System Navigation and Foundation Training
1. **Objective**: Familiarize users with CGI Advantage 4 interface, navigation, and general system functionality.
2. **Topics**:
   - Basic navigation: accessing modules, searching, and managing records.
   - Common fields, forms, and data entry standards across modules.
   - Overview of workflow and approval processes.

===== Module 2: Financial Management (FIN) Training
1. **Objective**: Train users on key financial functions within CGI Advantage 4.
2. **Topics**:
   - Accounts Payable: invoice entry, payment processing, and voucher reconciliation.
   - Accounts Receivable: billing, cash receipts, and customer account management.
   - General Ledger: journal entries, trial balance, and financial statement generation.
   - Budgeting: setting up budget structures, entering budgets, and monitoring budget controls.
3. **Hands-On Labs**: Practice entering invoices, creating journal entries, generating reports, and adjusting budgets.

===== Module 3: Human Resources Management (HRM) Training
1. **Objective**: Equip HR and payroll staff with skills for managing employee records, payroll, and benefits.
2. **Topics**:
   - Employee Profile: creating and updating employee records, managing personal and job data.
   - Payroll Processing: payroll run setup, tax configuration, and payroll calculation.
   - Benefits Management: enrolling employees, managing benefit plans, and handling benefit deductions.
   - Time and Attendance: entering time records, tracking leave balances, and leave request processing.
3. **Hands-On Labs**: Practice entering employee data, processing payroll, and managing benefits.

===== Module 4: Reporting and Advanced Functions
1. **Objective**: Teach users to generate, interpret, and analyze reports for informed decision-making.
2. **Topics**:
   - Standard Reports: financial statements, budget reports, payroll summaries, and HR reports.
   - Custom Reporting: using report tools to customize reports for specific needs.
   - Advanced Features: complex functions such as budget transfers, leave accruals, and benefits reconciliation.

==== Phase 3: Post-Training Support and Reinforcement

1. **Ongoing Support**:
   - Provide support through helpdesk resources, dedicated trainers, or designated "power users."
   - Offer drop-in sessions or “office hours” for users needing additional guidance.

2. **Job Aids and Manuals**:
   - Distribute quick reference guides, troubleshooting checklists, and workflow summaries to all users.
   - Make digital versions of all training materials accessible in an online repository.

3. **Evaluation and Feedback**:
   - Conduct follow-up assessments to gauge knowledge retention.
   - Collect user feedback on training effectiveness and areas for improvement.

4. **Refresher Courses**:
   - Offer periodic refresher courses and updates as CGI Advantage 4 is upgraded or as new features are added.

=== 4. Success Metrics and Evaluation

Evaluate training success based on user proficiency, system adoption, and feedback.

==== Key Metrics
- **User Proficiency**: Measured by post-training assessments and task completion rates.
- **System Adoption**: Observed through system usage metrics, such as the number of transactions processed.
- **Feedback Scores**: Gathered from surveys and user feedback, providing insight into training quality and areas for improvement.

==== Continuous Improvement
- Use evaluation data to continuously improve training materials and methods.
- Update training as CGI Advantage 4 features or modules are enhanced, ensuring training remains relevant and effective.

=== Summary
This training architecture, strategy, and plan are designed to enable end-users to leverage the full functionality of CGI Advantage 4’s FIN and HRM modules. Through structured learning paths, hands-on practice, and ongoing support, users will develop the skills necessary for efficient, accurate, and effective system utilization.
