=== 1. Financial Management Module

[cols="3,2,3,4,3", options="header"]
|===
| Component              | Type         | Key Tables/Transactions          | Dependencies/Relationships                                     | Baseline Artifacts/Data 

| General Ledger (GL)    | Core Module  | GL Account (GAC), GL Journal (GLJ) | Relies on Chart of Accounts (COA), Fund Codes, Department Codes | COA setup, Fiscal Year, Fund Codes 
| Accounts Payable (AP)  | Submodule    | AP Invoice (API), Vendor (VEN)     | Linked to Vendor Management, Cash Management                    | Vendor Profiles, Payment Terms 
| Accounts Receivable (AR) | Submodule  | AR Invoice (ARI), AR Payment (ARP) | Interfaces with Revenue Management                              | Customer Profiles, Payment Methods 
| Budgeting              | Submodule    | Budget Structure (BST), Budget Entry (BJE) | Relies on Fiscal Periods, GL Account Hierarchy | Budget Profiles, Budget Control Setup 
| Asset Management       | Submodule    | Asset Master (ASM), Depreciation (DEP) | Links to GL for asset transactions and depreciation             | Asset Categories, Depreciation Policies 
|===

=== Relationship Matrix Overview

[cols="3,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Modules | Primary Transactions and Tables | Dependent Components 

| General Ledger (GL) | GL Account (GAC), GL Journal (GLJ) | Accounts Payable, Revenue Entry, Asset Management, Reporting 
| Accounts Payable (AP) | AP Invoice (API), Vendor (VEN) | GL for expenditure tracking, Vendor Management 
| Accounts Receivable (AR) | AR Invoice (ARI), AR Payment (ARP) | Linked to Customer, Revenue Entry for collection purposes 
| Budgeting | Budget Structure (BST), Budget Control (BC) | Impacts GL, Procurement, Revenue transactions 
| Procurement | Purchase Order (POD), Vendor Management (VCC) | Budget Control, AP for encumbrance management 
| Revenue | Revenue Entry (RE), Customer Profile (CP) | Linked to Budgeting, AR, and Customer Billing Profiles 
| Payroll | Payroll Run (PRR), Employee Profile (EMP) | Dependent on HR employee setup, Budgeting for payroll expense 
|===
