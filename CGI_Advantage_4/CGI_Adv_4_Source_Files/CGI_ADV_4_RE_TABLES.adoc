=== Overview
This dataset covers the necessary tables and dependencies to configure an RE transaction where a primary customer receives services, but billing is directed to multiple third-party customers, each with their own billing address. The setup includes customer profiles, billing profiles, billing rate tables, and other related tables to ensure that all dependencies are met.

=== Process Overview

1. **Set Up Customer Profiles**: Create profiles for the primary customer and all third-party customers involved in the RE transaction.
2. **Define Billing Addresses**: Set up addresses for each third-party customer in the VAD table to ensure proper billing locations.
3. **Create Billing Profiles**: Define separate billing profiles in the Billing Profile Table, linking each third-party customer to the primary customer.
4. **Establish Customer Account Options**: Use CACT entries to specify third-party billing relationships and instructions.
5. **Set Rates in Billing Rate Table**: Define service rates and effective dates for billing calculations.
6. **Generate RE Transaction**: In the RE table, assign the billing profile ID for each line item to bill the correct third-party payer on behalf of the primary customer.

=== Notes

- Ensure that each billing profile in the Billing Profile Table references the correct third-party customer and address ID to prevent billing errors.
- Fiscal periods and account structures must be verified to ensure transactions align with reporting requirements.
- The Billing Rate Table should reflect updated rates to avoid miscalculations in the RE transaction.
