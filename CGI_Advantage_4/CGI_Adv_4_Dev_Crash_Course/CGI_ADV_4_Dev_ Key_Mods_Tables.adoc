=== Overview of Key Modules

CGI Advantage 4 is organized into a series of interconnected modules, each with its own dedicated tables that manage specific business processes. A thorough understanding of these modules and their core tables is essential for efficient development and customization.

<<<

=== Financial Management (FIN)

The Financial Management module encompasses the General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and Budgeting. These components form the backbone of financial operations in CGI Advantage 4.

==== General Ledger (GL)
The General Ledger is the central repository for financial transactions and provides the foundation for financial reporting.

- **Core Tables:**
  * `GL_ACCOUNT`: Stores information about accounts, including account numbers, descriptions, and types.
  * `GL_JOURNAL`: Tracks journal entries, including transaction details and source references.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Generating financial reports such as balance sheets and income statements.
  * Managing journal entries for auditing and reconciliation.

*Activity Suggestion:* Have students query the `GL_JOURNAL` table to retrieve transactions for a specific period.

<<<

==== Accounts Payable (AP)
The Accounts Payable module handles outgoing payments to vendors and suppliers, ensuring timely and accurate disbursements.

- **Key Tables:**
  * `AP_INVOICE`: Tracks vendor invoices, including amounts, due dates, and statuses.
  * `AP_PAYMENT`: Stores payment details, such as check numbers, payment methods, and amounts.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Automating invoice approvals and payment processing.
  * Generating reports on outstanding invoices and vendor payments.

*Instructor Note:* Highlight common relationships between `AP_INVOICE` and `AP_PAYMENT`.

<<<

==== Accounts Receivable (AR)
The Accounts Receivable module manages incoming payments from customers and other entities.

- **Core Tables:**
  * `AR_INVOICE`: Tracks customer invoices, including amounts and due dates.
  * `AR_PAYMENT`: Stores details of payments received, such as methods and dates.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Generating aging reports for overdue receivables.
  * Reconciling payments with invoices for financial accuracy.

<<<

==== Budgeting
The Budgeting component enables organizations to plan, allocate, and monitor financial resources.

- **Important Tables:**
  * `BUDGET_STRUCTURE`: Defines the hierarchy and structure of budgets, including categories and levels.
  * `BUDGET_ENTRY`: Tracks budget entries, including allocated and used amounts.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Monitoring budget adherence for specific departments or projects.
  * Analyzing variances between budgeted and actual amounts.

*Activity Suggestion:* Assign students to create sample queries to extract budget variance data from `BUDGET_ENTRY`.

<<<

=== Human Resources Management (HRM)

The Human Resources Management module provides tools for managing employee data, payroll, and benefits. These tables are critical for streamlining HR operations.

==== Employee Profile
Employee profile management involves maintaining personal and job-related information for all employees.

- **Core Tables:**
  * `EMPLOYEE_PROFILE`: Stores key employee data, including names, IDs, and employment statuses.
  * `EMPLOYEE_ADDRESS`: Maintains employee address information.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Generating employee directories and headcount reports.
  * Tracking employee status for compliance and reporting.

<<<

==== Payroll
Payroll management involves calculating and processing employee compensation, including taxes, benefits, and deductions.

- **Key Tables:**
  * `PAYROLL_RUN`: Logs payroll runs, including periods, totals, and statuses.
  * `PAY_CALCULATION`: Stores detailed payroll calculations, including individual earnings and deductions.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Analyzing payroll trends over time.
  * Ensuring compliance with tax regulations and labor laws.

*Instructor Note:* Discuss the integration between `PAYROLL_RUN` and `EMPLOYEE_PROFILE`.

<<<

=== Common Tables

Common tables in CGI Advantage 4 support multiple modules, providing shared data for streamlined operations.

==== Customer and Vendor Management
Customer and vendor tables centralize the profiles and interactions for these key stakeholders.

- **Key Tables:**
  * `CUSTOMER_PROFILE`: Stores customer data, including contact details and payment terms.
  * `VENDOR_PROFILE`: Maintains vendor information, such as names, addresses, and payment preferences.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Generating vendor performance reports.
  * Automating invoice generation for customers.

<<<

==== Account Options
The `ACCOUNT_OPTIONS` table plays a pivotal role in linking accounts with specific configurations, supporting customized financial operations.

- **Core Table:**
  * `ACCOUNT_OPTIONS`: Stores configuration settings tied to financial accounts.

- **Use Cases:**
  * Defining account-specific rules, such as tax codes or payment terms.
  * Customizing reporting views based on account configurations.

*Activity Suggestion:* Have students analyze the relationships between `ACCOUNT_OPTIONS` and other financial tables.

<<<

[appendix]
=== Glossary
- *General Ledger (GL):* A central system for managing financial transactions and reporting.
- *Accounts Payable (AP):* Tracks outgoing payments to vendors.
- *Accounts Receivable (AR):* Tracks incoming payments from customers.
- *Budgeting:* The process of planning and monitoring financial resources.
