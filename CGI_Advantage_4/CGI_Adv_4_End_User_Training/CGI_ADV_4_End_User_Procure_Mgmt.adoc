=== Overview of Procurement Management

The Procurement Management module in CGI Advantage 4 streamlines the process of acquiring goods and services while ensuring compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements.

- *Key Features:*
  * Requisitioning and approvals.
  * Vendor selection and performance tracking.
  * Contract management and compliance monitoring.
  * Purchase order creation and tracking.

*Instructor Note:* Highlight the importance of automation in reducing procurement cycle times and ensuring accuracy.

<<<

=== Procurement Lifecycle

==== Requisitioning
The requisitioning process initiates procurement by identifying organizational needs and creating requests for goods or services.

- **Steps to Create a Requisition:**
  1. Navigate to the "Requisitions" page.
  2. Select "New Requisition" and fill out required fields, including item descriptions, quantities, and cost estimates.
  3. Attach supporting documentation (e.g., specifications or justifications).
  4. Submit the requisition for approval.

- **Approval Process:**
  * Workflows route requisitions to the appropriate approvers based on thresholds or organizational policies.
  * Track the status of requisitions in real-time.

*Activity:* Create and submit a requisition for a new office supply order.

<<<

==== Vendor Selection
Vendor selection ensures that purchases are made from approved suppliers who meet organizational and regulatory standards.

- **Vendor Evaluation Criteria:**
  * Cost competitiveness.
  * Quality and reliability of goods or services.
  * Vendor compliance with certifications or regulations.

- **Vendor Selection Process:**
  1. Access the "Vendor Directory" to identify approved vendors.
  2. Compare vendor profiles and select the most suitable vendor.
  3. Document the selection rationale in the system.

*Instructor Tip:* Use a real-world scenario to demonstrate vendor comparison and selection.

<<<

==== Purchase Order Creation
Purchase orders (POs) formalize procurement requests and provide a legal document for transactions.

- **Steps to Create a PO:**
  1. Navigate to the "Purchase Orders" page and select "New PO."
  2. Populate fields such as vendor details, items, quantities, and delivery dates.
  3. Link the PO to a previously approved requisition if applicable.
  4. Submit the PO for approval and issue it to the vendor.

- **Tracking and Monitoring:**
  * Track the status of POs through their lifecycle, from creation to fulfillment.
  * Update POs with receipt and payment information as necessary.

*Activity:* Create a PO linked to an approved requisition and track its status.

<<<

=== Vendor and Contract Management

==== Vendor Management
Effective vendor management ensures reliable partnerships and compliance with organizational policies.

- **Vendor Profiles:**
  * Maintain detailed vendor profiles, including contact information, payment terms, and compliance documents.
  * Monitor vendor performance metrics, such as delivery times and quality ratings.

- **Vendor Onboarding:**
  * Register new vendors in the system using a standardized onboarding process.
  * Verify documentation, such as tax IDs, certifications, and insurance coverage.

*Activity:* Create and update a vendor profile for a new supplier.

<<<

==== Contract Management
The Contract Management feature enables organizations to create, manage, and monitor procurement contracts.

- **Creating Contracts:**
  1. Use pre-approved templates to draft contracts.
  2. Include key details, such as terms, conditions, and expiration dates.

- **Monitoring Contracts:**
  * Track contract performance using system-generated metrics.
  * Receive notifications for upcoming renewals or expirations.

- **Amending Contracts:**
  * Modify existing contracts to reflect changes in scope, pricing, or timelines.
  * Document amendments for audit purposes.

*Instructor Note:* Discuss the role of contracts in mitigating risks and ensuring accountability.

<<<

=== Compliance and Reporting

==== Ensuring Compliance
Compliance is critical in procurement to minimize risks and meet regulatory standards.

- **Compliance Measures:**
  * Ensure vendors meet certifications, such as minority-owned business certifications or environmental standards.
  * Document compliance checks within the system for audit purposes.

- **Audit Trails:**
  * Enable audit trails for all procurement activities, including approvals, modifications, and payments.
  * Generate compliance reports for internal and external audits.

<<<

==== Procurement Reporting
Reporting tools in CGI Advantage 4 provide insights into procurement activities and help identify areas for improvement.

- **Common Reports:**
  * Procurement Activity Report: Summarizes requisitions, POs, and contracts.
  * Vendor Performance Report: Tracks metrics like delivery accuracy and defect rates.
  * Budget Impact Report: Analyzes procurement spending against budgets.

- **Steps to Generate a Report:**
  1. Navigate to the "Reports" section within the Procurement Management module.
  2. Select the desired report type and apply relevant filters.
  3. Export the report in the preferred format for review or distribution.

*Activity:* Generate a Vendor Performance Report and interpret key metrics.

[appendix]
=== Glossary
- *Purchase Order (PO):* A document issued to a vendor to request goods or services.
- *Requisition:* A formal request for the purchase of goods or services.
- *Vendor Performance Metrics:* Quantifiable measures used to assess vendor reliability and quality.
