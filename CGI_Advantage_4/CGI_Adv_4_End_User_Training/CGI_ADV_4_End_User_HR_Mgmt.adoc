=== Overview of HR Management in CGI Advantage 4

The HRM module in CGI Advantage 4 streamlines workforce management by integrating employee data, payroll processing, and compliance reporting into a single platform.

- *Key Features:*
  * Employee lifecycle management, including hiring, onboarding, and terminations.
  * Payroll automation for accurate and timely employee compensation.
  * Benefits administration and compliance reporting.

*Instructor Note:* Highlight the importance of centralized HR data in reducing redundancy and improving decision-making.

<<<

=== Employee Lifecycle Management

==== Employee Profile Management
The Employee Profile feature is a centralized repository for managing employee data.

- **Adding a New Employee:**
  1. Navigate to the "Employee Profiles" section.
  2. Select "Add New Employee" and enter required information, such as name, job title, department, and start date.
  3. Attach necessary documents, such as offer letters or ID proof.

- **Updating Employee Information:**
  * Edit employee profiles to reflect changes, such as promotions or department transfers.
  * Maintain records of certifications, performance reviews, and training completions.

- **Terminating Employees:**
  1. Select the employee profile and choose "Terminate Employment."
  2. Specify the termination reason and effective date.
  3. Ensure all final payroll and benefits actions are completed.

*Activity:* Add a new employee profile and simulate a promotion process.

<<<

==== Onboarding and Offboarding
Streamlined onboarding and offboarding processes ensure a smooth transition for employees joining or leaving the organization.

- **Onboarding:**
  * Automate tasks such as assigning roles, providing system access, and scheduling training sessions.
  * Track onboarding progress through checklists and automated notifications.

- **Offboarding:**
  * Manage exit interviews, return of company assets, and final paychecks.
  * Generate reports on attrition rates and exit trends.

*Instructor Note:* Discuss how effective onboarding improves employee retention.

<<<

=== Payroll Management

==== Payroll Configuration
Payroll configuration ensures accurate and timely processing of employee wages.

- **Setting Up Payroll Schedules:**
  * Define pay periods (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly) and payment dates.
  * Assign pay schedules to specific employee groups.

- **Defining Payroll Rules:**
  * Configure pay elements such as base salary, overtime, bonuses, and deductions.
  * Set up tax calculations and compliance requirements based on local regulations.

*Activity:* Configure a payroll schedule and add deductions for a specific employee group.

<<<

==== Payroll Processing
Automating payroll processes reduces errors and ensures compliance with labor laws.

- **Steps for Payroll Processing:**
  1. Verify employee work hours and attendance records.
  2. Run payroll calculations to determine gross pay, deductions, and net pay.
  3. Generate payroll reports for approval.
  4. Process payments via direct deposit or checks.

- **Generating Payroll Reports:**
  * Create reports on payroll trends, tax withholdings, and benefits disbursement.
  * Use these reports for internal audits and compliance purposes.

*Activity:* Simulate a payroll run for a specific pay period and generate a summary report.

<<<

=== Benefits Administration

==== Managing Employee Benefits
The HRM module simplifies the administration of employee benefits, such as health insurance, retirement plans, and leave policies.

- **Benefits Configuration:**
  * Define benefit plans, including eligibility criteria and contribution structures.
  * Assign benefit plans to employee groups or individuals.

- **Tracking Benefits Usage:**
  * Monitor leave balances, retirement contributions, and insurance claims.
  * Generate reports to analyze benefits utilization trends.

*Instructor Note:* Discuss the importance of aligning benefits with employee needs and organizational goals.

<<<

=== HR Analytics and Reporting

==== Workforce Metrics
HR analytics provides insights into workforce trends, helping organizations make data-driven decisions.

- **Common Metrics:**
  * Employee turnover and retention rates.
  * Training and development participation.
  * Average time to fill open positions.

- **Steps to Generate Workforce Reports:**
  1. Navigate to the "Reports" section within the HRM module.
  2. Select a report type, such as "Turnover Analysis" or "Training Completion."
  3. Apply filters for specific departments, locations, or time periods.

*Activity:* Generate a turnover analysis report and interpret the results.

<<<

==== Compliance Reporting
Compliance reporting ensures adherence to labor laws and regulations.

- **Common Compliance Reports:**
  * Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) compliance reports.
  * Wage and Hour compliance summaries.
  * Tax filings and statutory contributions.

- **Automated Alerts:**
  * Set up notifications for upcoming compliance deadlines.
  * Automate the submission of regulatory filings where applicable.

*Activity:* Create a compliance report for EEO requirements and discuss potential areas for improvement.

=== Glossary
- *Onboarding:* The process of integrating a new employee into the organization.
- *Payroll Schedule:* A predefined timeline for processing and distributing employee wages.
- *Workforce Metrics:* Quantitative measures used to assess HR performance and trends.
