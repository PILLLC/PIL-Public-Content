=== Overview of Financial Management

The Financial Management module is the backbone of CGI Advantage 4, enabling organizations to manage finances, ensure compliance, and maintain transparency.

- *Key Features:*
  * General Ledger (GL) for tracking financial transactions.
  * Accounts Payable (AP) for managing vendor payments.
  * Accounts Receivable (AR) for tracking incoming payments.
  * Budgeting for financial planning and monitoring.

*Instructor Note:* Emphasize the importance of accurate data entry to ensure reliable financial reporting.

<<<

=== General Ledger (GL)

==== Purpose of the General Ledger
The General Ledger serves as the central repository for all financial transactions, providing the foundation for accurate reporting and compliance.

- *Key Functions:*
  * Record financial transactions through journal entries.
  * Maintain the chart of accounts (COA) for categorizing transactions.
  * Generate financial statements, such as income statements and balance sheets.

==== Performing Common Tasks
- **Entering Journal Entries:**
  1. Navigate to the "Journal Entries" page.
  2. Select "New Entry" and complete the required fields (e.g., account, amount, and description).
  3. Save and post the entry.

- **Reconciliation:**
  * Compare system transactions with bank statements to ensure accuracy.
  * Use the reconciliation tool to match entries and resolve discrepancies.

*Activity:* Practice entering and posting a sample journal entry in a training environment.

<<<

=== Accounts Payable (AP)

==== Managing Vendor Payments
Accounts Payable tracks outgoing payments to vendors, ensuring timely and accurate processing.

- *Key Tasks:*
  * **Invoice Processing:**
    1. Create a new invoice by entering vendor details, amounts, and payment terms.
    2. Attach supporting documentation, such as purchase orders or contracts.
    3. Submit the invoice for approval.
  * **Payment Processing:**
    1. Generate payment batches for approved invoices.
    2. Print checks or process electronic fund transfers (EFTs).
    3. Record payment details in the system.

==== Vendor Management
- Maintain vendor profiles, including payment terms, contact information, and compliance documentation.
- Monitor vendor performance using system-generated metrics and reports.

*Activity:* Process a sample invoice and generate a payment batch for review.

<<<

=== Accounts Receivable (AR)

==== Tracking Incoming Payments
Accounts Receivable ensures that all payments owed to the organization are tracked and processed accurately.

- *Key Tasks:*
  * **Invoice Creation:**
    1. Generate customer invoices based on services rendered or goods provided.
    2. Include due dates and payment terms in the invoice.
  * **Payment Application:**
    1. Record payments received via checks, EFTs, or other methods.
    2. Match payments to corresponding invoices to close balances.

==== Collections Management
- Monitor overdue accounts and generate follow-up notices.
- Implement escalation workflows for unresolved payments.

*Activity:* Create a customer invoice and apply a payment in a training environment.

<<<

=== Budgeting and Reporting

==== Budget Management
The Budgeting feature allows users to plan, monitor, and adjust financial resources effectively.

- *Creating Budgets:*
  1. Navigate to the "Budget Setup" page.
  2. Define budget categories, such as departments or projects.
  3. Enter initial budget amounts and save the configuration.

- *Monitoring Performance:*
  * Use the "Budget vs. Actual" report to compare planned and actual expenditures.
  * Analyze variances to identify areas for adjustment.

*Activity:* Set up a departmental budget and generate a variance report.

<<<

==== Financial Reporting
Generate detailed reports to provide insights into financial performance and compliance.

- *Common Reports:*
  * Trial Balance
  * Income Statement
  * Budget vs. Actual Analysis
  * Vendor Payment Summary

- *Steps to Generate a Report:*
  1. Navigate to the "Reports" section in the Financial Management module.
  2. Select the desired report type and apply filters (e.g., date range, account).
  3. Generate and export the report in the desired format (PDF, Excel, etc.).

*Activity:* Generate and review a Trial Balance report.

<<<

[appendix]
=== Glossary
- *General Ledger (GL):* A system for recording all financial transactions.
- *Accounts Payable (AP):* Manages outgoing payments to vendors.
- *Accounts Receivable (AR):* Tracks incoming payments from customers or other entities.
- *Budget vs. Actual Report:* Compares planned expenditures against actual spending.
