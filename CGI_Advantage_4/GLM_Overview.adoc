== How to Use This Document

This document is a *trainer reference*, not a slide script.

It is designed to:
- Sit beside the delivery deck during live demos
- Provide exact transaction/page intent and business rules
- Support unscripted questions without derailing flow
- Preserve institutional knowledge across GLM stages

GLM should be positioned as the **system of record for grant intent, decisions, and compliance**, distinct from budgeting, accounting, and payments.

---

== GLM Lifecycle Overview

GLM tracks the full lifecycle of a grant from *opportunity awareness* through *close-out and audit*.

The lifecycle progresses through seven stages:

. Opportunity Identification
. Response Decision
. Application
. Award
. Post-Award Alignment
. Amendment
. Close-Out / Audit

The lifecycle is anchored by the *Grant ID*, which is created at application and persists through award, amendment, and close-out.

All stages roll up into the *Grant Tracking Summary (GRNTSUM)* inquiry.

= SESSION 1 (45 Minutes)
== GLM Overview + Stage 1: Opportunity Identification

=== Session Purpose

Introduce GLM as a lifecycle tracking system that answers:
- Why a grant exists
- Who made decisions
- When actions occurred
- What obligations were created

Emphasize that GLM:
- Does *not* create budgets
- Does *not* post accounting entries
- Does provide authoritative audit context

---

== Stage 1 – Opportunity Identification

=== GTAOD – Available Grant Opportunity Detail

Purpose::
Stores grant opportunity data imported from Grants.gov.

Key Characteristics::
- Loaded via batch (Update Grant Opportunity)
- Read-only reference
- External data source
- Not a lifecycle object

Trainer Emphasis::
This is *intelligence ingestion*, not internal decision-making.

---

=== GTAOS – Available Grant Opportunity Summary

Purpose::
Search and summarize available opportunities.

Key Characteristics::
- Search/filter entry point
- Launch point for creating GTOP
- Used by agencies to triage opportunities

Trainer Emphasis::
This is where agencies decide *whether* to engage.

---

=== GTOP – Grant Opportunity (Document)

Purpose::
Creates the internal representation of a grant opportunity.

Creates / Updates::
- GTOPSF (Opportunity Status Folder)
- GTNTSF (Notification Status Folder)

Key Characteristics::
- Captures opportunity ownership
- Establishes internal accountability
- Enables notifications and alerts

---

=== GTOPSF – Grant Opportunity Status Folder

Core Sections::
- General Information
- Funding Agency
- Funding Agency Contact
- Pass-Through Agency
- Comments

Business Rules::
- Department required
- Opportunity ID must be unique
- Funding agency data drives downstream award context

Trainer Emphasis::
GTOPSF is where an opportunity becomes *institutional knowledge*.

---

=== GTNTSF – Grant Notification Status Folder

Purpose::
Identifies who is notified about the opportunity.

Key Characteristics::
- Uses Grant Users or Alert User Groups
- Drives alerts and reminders
- Supports governance and accountability

Trainer Emphasis::
Notifications are not just emails—they drive workflow discipline.

---

=== Batch Process – Update Grant Opportunity

Purpose::
Imports Grants.gov XML into Advantage.

Key Characteristics::
- Updates GTAOD and GTAOS
- Typically centrally scheduled
- Does not create lifecycle records

---

=== Session 1 Demo Flow (Suggested)

. Search GTAOS
. Drill into GTAOD
. Create GTOP
. Walk GTOPSF tabs
. Review GTNTSF recipients
. Preview lifecycle in GRNTSUM

= SESSION 2 (45 Minutes)
== Stage 2: Response + Stage 3: Application

=== Session Framing

Stages 2 and 3 answer:
- Are we applying?
- What did we submit?

This is where *governance* turns into *execution*.

---

== Stage 2 – Response

=== GTRE – Grant Response (Document)

Purpose::
Records the agency’s decision to apply or not apply.

Updates::
- GTRESF

---

=== GTRESF – Grant Response Status Folder

Tracks::
- Apply / Not Apply decision
- Decline reason
- Decision date

Business Rules::
- Decision required
- Decline reason required if “Not Apply”
- Completion date required once response entered

Trainer Emphasis::
GTRESF preserves decision history and prevents silent drop-off.

---

== Stage 3 – Application

=== GTAP – Grant Application (Document)

Purpose::
Creates the formal grant application record.

Updates::
- GTAPSF

Critical Concept::
The *Grant ID is created here* and persists for the full lifecycle.

---

=== GTAPSF – Grant Application Status Folder

Core Sections::
- General Information
- Contact Information
- Pre-Application Information
- Application Information
- Supporting Agencies
- Application Dates
- Comments

Key Business Rules::
- Grant ID is mandatory and persistent
- Contact information is required
- Date rows require a grant date code
- Date templates reduce audit risk

Trainer Emphasis::
Opportunity ID and Grant ID are not the same—and serve different purposes.

---

=== Session 2 Demo Flow (Suggested)

. Open GTRESF and record Apply decision
. Create GTAP
. Walk GTAPSF sections
. Emphasize Grant ID continuity
. Review lifecycle status in GRNTSUM

---

= SESSION 3 (45 Minutes)
== Stage 4: Award + Stage 5: Post-Award Alignment

=== Session Framing

Stage 4 converts intent into obligation.  
Stage 5 positions GLM as the authoritative context provider.

---

== Stage 4 – Award

=== GTAW – Grant Award (Document)

Purpose::
Records award details and acceptance.

Updates::
- GTAWSF

---

=== GTAWSF – Grant Award Status Folder

Core Sections::
- General Information
- Grant Award Information
- Agreement Information
- Award Administrator Contact
- Award Dates
- Comments

Critical Business Rules::
- Awarded Amount required
- Award Notification Date required
- Awarded amount becomes amendment baseline

Trainer Emphasis::
Awards lock history; amendments layer changes.

---

== Stage 5 – Post-Award Alignment

What GLM Does Not Do::
- Create budgets
- Distribute funds
- Post accounting transactions

What GLM Does::
- Maintain authoritative award record
- Provide reporting and audit context
- Integrate with Cost Accounting and compliance processes

Trainer Positioning::
GLM explains *why* money exists—not how it moves.

---

=== Session 3 Demo Flow (Suggested)

. Create GTAW from application
. Walk GTAWSF tabs
. Highlight awarded amount rules
. Explain downstream integration
. Review GRNTSUM award status

---

= SESSION 4 (45 Minutes)
== Stage 6: Amendment + Stage 7: Close-Out / Audit

=== Session Framing

Most grant risk lives in change management and closure.

---

== Stage 6 – Amendment

=== GTAM – Grant Amendment (Document)

Purpose::
Records changes to an awarded grant.

Updates::
- GTAMSF

---

=== GTAMSF – Grant Amendment Status Folder

Amendment Types::
- Dollar change
- End date change
- Scope change
- Termination
- Return of funds

Business Rules::
- Amendment type drives required fields
- Amendments do not overwrite award history
- Awarded amount becomes cumulative

Trainer Emphasis::
Amendments preserve intent and chronology.

---

== Stage 7 – Close-Out and Audit

=== GTCO – Grant Close-Out / Audit (Document)

Purpose::
Records audit completion and closes the grant.

Updates::
- GTCOSF

---

=== GTCOSF – Grant Close-Out / Audit Status Folder

Tracks::
- Audit status
- Final reporting
- Grant closure

System Behavior::
- Close Grant may inactivate grant-related accounting codes
- Opportunity lifecycle is finalized

Trainer Emphasis::
Close-out is a control point, not paperwork.

---

=== Alerts and Governance

=== Generate Alerts (Batch)

Purpose::
Sends reminders for:
- Missing responses
- Incomplete applications
- Reporting deadlines
- Pending close-outs

Trainer Emphasis::
Alerts reduce institutional risk and reliance on memory.

---

=== Session 4 Demo Flow (Suggested)

. Open GTAMSF and apply amendment
. Show cumulative impact
. Create GTCO
. Walk GTCOSF
. Review alert generation
. Final GRNTSUM walkthrough

---

== Final Trainer Reminder

GLM is the system of record for:
- Grant intent
- Decisions
- Obligations
- Compliance

It provides auditability, governance, and continuity across administrations, staff turnover, and funding cycles.
