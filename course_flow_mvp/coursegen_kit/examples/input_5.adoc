= Fundamentals of Modern Physics
:toc:

== Objective
To provide a comprehensive understanding of the key concepts and principles of modern physics, including relativity, quantum mechanics, and atomic structure.

== Description
This course covers the essential topics in modern physics, focusing on the theories and experiments that have shaped our understanding of the physical universe. Students will explore the implications of Einstein's theories, the principles of quantum mechanics, and the behavior of matter at the atomic and subatomic levels.

== Introduction to Modern Physics
An overview of the historical context and key developments that led to the emergence of modern physics.

=== The Birth of Modern Physics
Explore the transition from classical to modern physics and the key figures involved.

=== Key Experiments in Modern Physics
Examine pivotal experiments that shaped modern physics, including the photoelectric effect and the double-slit experiment.

=== Theoretical Foundations
Understand the fundamental theories that underpin modern physics, including relativity and quantum theory.

== Special Relativity
A deep dive into Einstein's theory of special relativity and its implications for space and time.

=== Postulates of Special Relativity
Learn about the two key postulates that form the basis of special relativity.

=== Time Dilation and Length Contraction
Understand the concepts of time dilation and length contraction and their experimental confirmations.

=== Mass-Energy Equivalence
Explore the famous equation E=mc² and its significance in modern physics.

== Quantum Mechanics
An introduction to the principles and applications of quantum mechanics.

=== Wave-Particle Duality
Investigate the dual nature of light and matter and its implications for quantum theory.

=== Quantum States and Superposition
Learn about quantum states, superposition, and their role in quantum mechanics.

=== Uncertainty Principle
Understand Heisenberg's uncertainty principle and its impact on our understanding of measurement in quantum mechanics.

== Atomic Structure
A study of the structure of atoms and the forces that govern their behavior.

=== The Bohr Model of the Atom
Explore the Bohr model and its explanation of atomic spectra.

=== Quantum Mechanical Model of the Atom
Understand the quantum mechanical model and the concept of electron orbitals.

=== Periodic Table and Electron Configuration
Learn how the periodic table is organized based on atomic structure and electron configuration.

== Applications of Modern Physics
Examine the practical applications of modern physics in technology and research.

=== Quantum Computing
Discover the principles of quantum computing and its potential to revolutionize technology.

=== Nuclear Physics and Energy
Explore the principles of nuclear physics and its applications in energy production.

=== Modern Physics in Medicine
Learn about the applications of modern physics in medical imaging and treatment.
