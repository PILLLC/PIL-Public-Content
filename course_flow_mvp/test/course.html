Module 01: Course Overview





Learning Objectives

Upon completion of this module, learners will be able to:

  • Understand Objective

  • Understand Description

Objective

To empower divorced dads to embrace a fulfilling lifestyle that balances leisure, personal growth, and financial stability.

Description

This course provides practical strategies and insights for divorced fathers to transition into a life of leisure and luxury. Participants will learn how to manage their time, finances, and personal well-being while enjoying the benefits of their new lifestyle.
