== Module 1: Introduction to NIST and the RMF

.Objectives:
- To introduce the history and role of NIST in cybersecurity.
- To explain the RMF and its significance.
- To demonstrate the application of RMF in achieving compliance and security improvements.

.Lessons:
- The history of NIST and its impact on global cybersecurity standards.
- An overview of the RMF process and its components.
- The importance of RMF in various sectors and its role in regulatory compliance.

== Module 2: Understanding the RMF Process

.Objectives:
- To detail the steps involved in the RMF process.
- To illustrate RMF's integration into system development.
- To highlight the role of each RMF step in risk mitigation.

.Lessons:
- Explanation of the RMF's seven-step process.
- Application of RMF throughout the system development lifecycle.
- Real-world applications and benefits of the RMF process.

== Module 3: Preparing for RMF

.Objectives:

- Overview of key roles and responsibilities in the RMF process.
- Techniques for establishing and understanding the risk management context.
